About the shop

We treat every vehicle like it's our own.

Blue Collar Autobody is run by Rohin Buttar, a Red Seal certified autobody technician. That means your repair is handled by someone who meets the highest trade standard in Canada.

We don't cut corners. We take the time to do the job right the first time, so your vehicle leaves looking and driving the way it should.

Our services

Everything from a chip to a full respray.

One shop for small cosmetic fixes, major collision work, and custom paint. Not sure what you need? Send a photo and ask.

Text us a photo →
  1. 01

    Collision repair

    Minor fender benders or major structural damage, we restore your vehicle to factory condition. We assess the damage carefully, explain your options, and get you back on the road.

  2. 02

    Panel replacement & repair

    Dented, creased, or damaged panels get repaired wherever possible to save you money. When a panel is beyond repair, we replace it and blend it so it fits and matches perfectly.

  3. 03

    Custom paintwork

    Full resprays, colour changes, two-tone finishes, or custom designs. Quality paint systems and careful prep for a smooth, lasting finish that turns heads.

  4. 04

    Colour matching & spot repairs

    Scratches, chips, and scuffs get blended into your existing paint so the repair disappears.

  5. 05

    Fibreglass repair

    Cracked or damaged fibreglass on body kits, bumpers, boats, RVs, and trailers, restored for strength and appearance.

  6. 06

    Bumper repair

    Cracked, scuffed, or pushed-in bumpers repaired and refinished, often without the cost of a full replacement.

  7. 07

    Dent repair

    From door dings to larger dents, we bring panels back to their original shape.

  8. 08

    Rust repair

    We cut out the rust, repair the metal, then seal and refinish the area to stop it coming back.

  9. 09

    Polishing & paint correction

    Machine polishing removes swirl marks, oxidation, and light scratches to bring back a deep, glossy shine.

  10. 10

    Headlight restoration

    Foggy, yellowed headlights restored to clear, for better looks and better visibility at night.
